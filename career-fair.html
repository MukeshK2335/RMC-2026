Connecting Talent
with Opportunities

Career Fair 2026 is a virtual talent engagement and employment initiative designed to connect professionals, career returners, graduates, and job seekers with employers, recruiters, and industry leaders from around the world.

Organized as part of the Returning Mothers Conference Initiative, the Career Fair is conducted in collaboration with the IEEE Industrial Engagement Committee, creating meaningful opportunities for workforce development, professional networking, career readiness, and employment. Participants will have the opportunity to engage with employers, explore career pathways, enhance their professional skills, and access employment opportunities through virtual Career Fair events during 2026.

A PATHWAY BACK TO ENGINEERING

Returning Women Engineers
Program (RWEP)

Supporting women engineers in confidently returning to technical careers through mentorship, training, and industry engagement.

01 THE PROGRAM

The Returning Women Engineers Program (RWEP) is a comprehensive initiative designed to support women engineers in re-entering technical careers.

Funded by Quest Global and IEEE India Philanthropy, the program equips participants with the knowledge, confidence, and professional support needed to reconnect with engineering and technology careers.
