A celebration of new beginnings

About IEEE RMDay

IEEE Returning Mothers’ Day celebrates women returning to their professional journeys after a career break. It recognizes their experience, resilience, and potential, while bringing together a community ready to encourage and support their next steps.

Through connection, shared stories, and opportunities to learn, RMDay helps make the return to work more welcoming for mothers, employers, mentors, and allies.

01 · About the conference

What is IEEE RMC?

IEEE Returning Mothers Conference (RMC) supports women returning to professional life after a career break. The conference brings mothers, employers, mentors, and allies together for connection, learning, and practical support for the next step in their careers.

Learn about IEEE RMC ↗

02 · Who it is for

Who should attend?

Returning mothers at any stage of their career journey are at the heart of RMC. Employers, recruiters, educators, mentors, IEEE members, and anyone working to create more inclusive workplaces are warmly invited too.

03 · What you will gain

Why should I attend?

Meet people who understand the return-to-work journey, exchange experiences, discover useful skills and resources, and build relationships that can support your next career chapter. Employers and allies can hear new perspectives and explore ways to make work more welcoming.

04 · Event details

When and where?

IEEE RMC 2026 takes place on 13–14 November 2026 at NMIT, Bengaluru, India. Join us for two days of conversations, learning, collaboration, and celebration.

05 · Get involved

What can I participate in?

Take part in Returning Mothers’ Day, the Ideathon & Pitchfest, community conversations, and sessions across health and wellness, communication and branding, and sustainability and ethics. The program spans both days, with more session details to come.
