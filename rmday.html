About IEEE RM Day

In today’s fast-paced world, more women are breaking barriers, pursuing higher education, and building successful careers. However, many face the difficult choice of stepping back from their careers after marriage and motherhood to care for their families. Upon seeking to return to the workforce, they often encounter resistance from family members and challenges in regaining career momentum.

Since 2014, the Returning Mothers Conference has been empowering women to restart their professional journeys with confidence. In collaboration with IEEE and the WePOWER network of the World Bank, we jointly initiated the idea of Returning Mothers Day.

The International Returning Mothers Day was officially launched during the IEEE Returning Mothers Conference on November 15, 2024, at IISc Bangalore. This global celebration marks a significant step in supporting and empowering mothers returning to the workforce.

What is Returning Mothers Day?

Returning Mothers Day is a global initiative dedicated to recognizing, celebrating, and supporting women who have taken a break from their careers for motherhood and are now reentering the workforce. This day aims to create awareness around the challenges returning mothers face and the incredible value they bring to workplaces.

Why is it Important?

The transition back to work can be overwhelming for many mothers, often filled with hurdles such as regaining career momentum, balancing family responsibilities, and re-establishing their professional identity. By acknowledging this journey, Returning Mothers Day provides a platform to advocate for more inclusive work environments, flexible policies, and increased support for women resuming their careers after a break.

Key Objectives and
Benefits

Empowerment and Recognition

Celebrating the resilience, skills, and contributions of returning mothers, boosting their confidence and sense of value.

Inclusive Workplaces

Encouraging organizations to adopt policies that foster diversity and inclusivity.

Talent Retention

Helping businesses tap into an experienced talent pool, reducing hiring costs, and enhancing productivity.

Skill Development

Promoting continuous learning and professional growth through workshops and mentorship opportunities.

Stronger Communities

Building a supportive network where returning mothers can share experiences and grow together.

Cultural Shift

Breaking stereotypes around career breaks and encouraging positive societal attitudes toward working mothers.
